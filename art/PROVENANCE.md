# Instrument artwork

All SVG, PNG, model JSON, and item art in this project was authored for ModularCraft
by the implementation agent on 2026-11-03. No Minecraft textures, third-party images,
Blender models, generated images, or downloaded fonts were copied. License: MIT,
matching the repository. `scripts/generate-assets.py` is the editable procedural
source; SVGs are editable drawings; deterministic PNGs and Minecraft block/item JSON
are the shipped outputs. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/generate-assets.py`.

The charcoal steel housings share fasteners and ventilation; saw trace, low-pass
response, ADSR curve, and speaker grille identify the four modules independently of
color. Port labels AO/AI/CO/CI/GI and cable arrowheads supplement audio/control colors.
Full port names are displayed by the patching-tool HUD. The upper panel is decorative;
right-click opens the functional parameter editor. Native visual acceptance is separate
from asset generation and remains tracked in `validation/ledger.md`.

Keyboard extension: original piano motif and CV/gate socket labels generated by the same deterministic script; oscillator/envelope faces include matching pitch/gate input sockets. No external assets.


The Euclidean Sequencer front is original code-authored SVG/PNG: four labeled rings and
seven jacks, generated by `scripts/generate-assets.py`. Live markers are original client
geometry. Euclid-O-Matic and Euclidean Circles inform musical controls only; no firmware,
manual artwork, logos, or third-party textures are copied or bundled.


Square oscillator extension (2026-11-04): original procedural SQR panel, square-wave trace,
matching cyan saw accent/chassis and AO/PI sockets. Editable SVG and deterministic 64px PNG
come from the same script; no external artwork or dependencies.

LFO/CV extension (2026-11-05): original procedural lavender LFO sine-trace panel with
CO/CI sockets; filter gains RC and envelope gains A/D/S/R socket labels. Editable SVG,
deterministic 64px PNG, block/item models and loot use the existing generator and MIT
license. No external assets, fonts, image services, or dependency changes.

Audio Mixer (2026-11-05): original code-authored MIX panel with five fader motifs, cyan
input jacks 1–4 and AO, shared chassis and master accent. Editable SVG and deterministic
64px PNG originate in generate-assets.py under the existing MIT license. No external art.


The Amp/Speaker split retains the original registry name `speaker` for the revised Amp
face (AI/GI/GT/AO and an ADSR contour), and adds `audio_speaker` for the dedicated
speaker-grille face (AI only). Both editable SVGs and 64×64 PNGs are generated by
`scripts/generate-assets.py` using the same original geometric artwork and palette.
No third-party artwork or new asset dependencies were added.

Step Sequencer (2026-11-07): original STEP panel with sixteen rectangular indicators and
CV/GO/CI/RI/CO sockets. Editable SVG and deterministic 64px PNG are authored by the existing
Python generator under the repository license. No external artwork or dependencies.
